Transforming the Agent Experience: A Redesigned Portal for World Financial Group (WFG)

A streamlined design to enhance usability, boost productivity, and empower financial professionals.

The redesign of WFG’s agent portal improved usability and productivity through user research, prototyping, and a customized design system. The project aimed to enhance task completion rates, user satisfaction, and engagement.

My Role

In this project, I focused on addressing the UX challenges faced by licensed agents. I participated in user interviews, surveys, and usability tests to gather data and understand user needs. My goal was to represent typical users and brainstorm solutions to enhance ease of access, personalization, and task prioritization. I created low-fidelity wireframes and high-fidelity prototypes, and led multiple rounds of usability testing to refine the designs. Additionally, I played a key role in developing a customized design system to meet WFG’s unique requirements, ensuring consistency and usability across the portal.

The Problem

Licensed agents are struggling with their current homepage. The layout is not intuitive, and essential tools are hard to access. This lack of usability leads to frustration and reduced productivity. The homepage fails to cater to individual business needs, making it difficult for agents to quickly find information and navigate relevant tools.

The Challenge

The main UX challenges include difficulty in quickly accessing information, navigating relevant tools, and a lack of personalization to align with individual business needs. Additionally, WFG had unique design requirements that necessitated the development of a customized design system.

The Journey

User Research

To understand the depth of these issues, we embarked on a comprehensive research journey. We conducted user interviews with licensed agents and senior agents (MD/SMD levels) to understand their workflow, needs, and pain points. Broader user feedback was captured through surveys, highlighting existing portal pain points and desired features. Usability tests were performed on the current portal to pinpoint specific friction points in navigation and task completion. An analysis of similar portals was conducted to identify features that enhance usability and user engagement.

From our research, we identified the licensed agent needs quick access to sales tools and resources, while the senior agent requires oversight of team metrics and reports.

Ideation and Design

With a clear understanding of user needs, we brainstormed solutions. Brainstorming sessions generated various layout concepts to enhance ease of access, personalization, and task prioritization. We created low-fidelity wireframes and high-fidelity prototypes to visualize the design solutions, including an interactive dashboard and personalized content sections. Given WFG’s unique design requirements, we also developed a customized design system to ensure consistency and meet their specific needs.

Testing and Iteration

Our prototypes underwent rigorous testing. Multiple rounds of usability testing with both low-fidelity and high-fidelity prototypes helped identify usability issues and gather user feedback. Based on feedback, we iterated on the prototypes, improving button placements, color schemes, and making essential tools more prominent. Customization options were enhanced to allow users to prioritize their most-used tools and resources.

The Outcomes

Presenting Results and Impact

The redesigned homepage aimed to achieve the following outcomes. We aimed for a 95% task completion rate, indicating improved navigation and ease of access. We targeted a 20% increase in weekly logins, reflecting better user engagement. We strived for a 75% satisfaction rate, showing a positive response to the redesign. We set a goal for an NPS score of 40+, indicating user likelihood to recommend the portal.

To showcase the impact, we included final high-fidelity design screenshots, user flows, and diagrams of the dashboard layout changes. Before-and-after visuals highlighted improvements in layout, navigation, and user experience.

Key Takeaways and Learnings

Reflecting on the process, this project reinforced the importance of user-centered design, particularly in identifying and addressing pain points through direct user feedback. The iterative design process highlighted the value of usability testing and rapid prototyping in refining solutions based on real user input.

Future Considerations

Looking ahead, further customization options could enhance user satisfaction, allowing agents to adjust their homepage layout based on their role or preferences. Future research could explore integrating AI to streamline task prioritization and improve productivity.
